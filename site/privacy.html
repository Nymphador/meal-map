Meal Map privacy policy

Effective 6 October 2026

Meal Map is a meal planner, recipe box and shopping list for Android. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it. In short: everything you put into the app stays on your phone. We have no servers, no accounts and never see your data. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information as described below.

Information you put into the app

Your recipes, photos, ingredient prices, meal plans, pantry, shopping lists and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, which Google stores for you under its own privacy policy. When you use "Save a backup", the file goes wherever you choose to save or send it.

Importing recipes

When you import a recipe from a web link, the app downloads that page (and its photo) directly from the website you entered, in the same way a web browser would. That website receives a normal web request from your phone. Recipe PDFs and pasted text are read on your device and never uploaded.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad and, if you choose to watch one, a short video ad. These come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • information about ads shown and your interactions with them (for example, views and taps)
  • diagnostic and performance information

Depending on where you live and the choices you make, ads may be personalised. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app asks for your consent before ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings (Google → Ads). Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Buying Meal Map Premium turns ads off, and AdMob is no longer used by the app.

Purchases

The Premium purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your account has bought Premium, so it can turn ads off and unlock its features.

Children

Meal Map is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app, which removes it from the phone. Data in an Android device backup is managed in your Google account settings. Ad-related data collected by Google is kept and deleted according to Google's policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
